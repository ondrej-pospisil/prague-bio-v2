8 sessions that will transform your career

Gain a complete management skill set for leadership roles.

  1. 011 Oct 2026

    Building a Business

    Market analysis, product-market fit, customer validation.

    You will learn to identify market opportunities and validate business ideas directly with customers.

  2. 0215 Oct 2026

    Business Plan & Corporate Finance

    Financial planning, business models, unit economics.

    You will understand how startup finances work and how to put together a realistic business plan.

  3. 0326 Oct 2026

    IP protection & Regulatory

    Intellectual property, patents, regulatory environment.

    You will learn about IP protection and regulatory requirements in science and research.

  4. 0412 Nov 2026

    Legal Minimum for Start-up Management

    Legal minimum for running a startup.

    You will gain the basics of corporate law, contracts, and management responsibility.

  5. 0526 Nov 2026

    Business Development & Marketing Techniques

    B2B marketing, sales strategy, business development.

    You will learn how to get your product to market and attract your first customers.

  6. 0610 Dec 2026

    Team Competences, Dynamics and Leadership

    Team building, leadership, corporate culture.

    You will acquire practical skills for managing people and building teams.

  7. 077 Jan 2027

    Negotiation with Investors & Access to Public Support

    Obtaining investments, negotiating with investors.

    You will learn where to look for investors and how to deal with them.

  8. 0821 Jan 2027

    Pitch Deck & Presentation

    Presentation skills, storytelling, building your investor pitch deck.

    You will create an investor pitch deck, learn how to present and capture investors‘ attention.

  9. 094 Feb 2027

    Wrap-up Event

    Final pitch deck.

    You will present your project to investors, answer their toughest questions, and receive your Executive Academy certificate.

What do you get?

  • A systematic path to a management position
  • Long-term mentoring
  • Network of contacts with investors, mentors, and classmates
  • Access to the biotech community and subsequent events
  • Certificate from Prague.bio
  • Possibility of an internship at one of our partner projects
Apply now

Who is the program intended for?

  • Post-docs and PhD students

    in biotech / life science-related fields with an interest and motivation to connect scientific knowledge with the commercial sphere

  • Scientists

    considering transitioning into technology company management or running their own company

  • Researchers and scientists

    who want to build a career in innovation management, technology transfer or science-based business.

Executive Academy is about people, not projects.

You don’t need a startup idea to join.

Step by step

  1. 01

    Submit an Application

    The deadline is 31 July 2026. We will confirm receipt of your application immediately.

  2. 02

    Jury Meeting

    On 10 August 2026, the jury will select 12 participants. We will let you know the results by 16 August.

  3. 03

    Payment

    We expect payment for the Academy by 31 August. We will confirm receipt of your payment.

  4. 04

    Kick-off Meeting

    On 24 September 2026, in the early evening, we will meet in person for the first time and get to know each other.

  5. 05

    Face-to-face Workshops

    We start on 1 October 2026. We will meet twice a month, usually on Thursdays from 4 to 8 PM.

  6. 06

    Wrap-up Event

    We will conclude our joint work on 4 February 2027 with a celebratory evening and a pitch competition.

Application Form

Applications for the upcoming Executive Academy are now closed. The next edition will take place in spring 2027. If you are interested in joining, feel free to get in touch already – applications will officially open in October. Follow Prague.bio on LinkedIn for updates and further information. Thank you.

In your CV kindly remember to mention:

  • Highest level of your education (university, field of study),
  • internships and study stays,
  • current institution / employer and your academic position,
  • the subject of your research.

In your motivation letter, please describe:

  • Why are you applying for the programme?
  • How do you expect the academy will support your career development…? (max. 3000 characters)

Please attach your CV and a motivation letter in English. Accepted formats: PDF, DOC, DOCX · Max. 5 MB
