Prague.bio gathers and connects key players in the life sciences sector in the Czech Republic and the region of Central Europe. Our members range from large pharmaceutical companies and startups to R&D institutions and public bodies.

Why join Prague.Bio?

  • Be part of a growing community of biotech leaders

    We currently have over 40 members, ranging from large pharmaceutical companies and startups to R&D institutions and government bodies.

  • Expand your network

    We encourage our members to connect and collaborate. Throughout the year, we organize events and meetings to foster meaningful relationships within the biotech community.

  • Find the right partners

    Looking for matchmaking opportunities, investors, international partners? We can help you find the right connections.

  • Make your voice heard

    The biotech sector in the Czech Republic has been somewhat fragmented, and we aim to unify its voice. This strengthens our position when engaging with government bodies and other key stakeholders.

  • Boost your visibility

    Increase your presence in the biotech sector through our platform, events, and collaborations.

  • Exclusive access to local & international events

    Gain first-hand access to events organized by Prague.bio, as well as major international conferences where we offer opportunities for our members—such as exhibiting at our stand.

How to become a member

  1. 01

    Contact us

    If you’re interested in joining Prague.bio, please reach out via the contact form below.

  2. 02

    Submit a formal application

    After the initial contact, we will send you a formal application form where you can provide all relevant details about your company or institution.

  3. 03

    Approval by the Prague.bio Executive board

    Once we receive your application, our Executive board will review and evaluate it for approval.

  4. 04

    Membership fee

    If your application is approved, you will be required to pay the membership fee according to our price list.

  5. 05

    Welcome to Prague.bio!

    Congratulations! You are now a member of the Prague.bio community. We look forward to collaborating with you!
