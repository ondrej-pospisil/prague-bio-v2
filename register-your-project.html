Register your project
for pitch and poster session

Pitch and Poster Session are suitable for start-ups/spin-offs ideally in pre-seed or seed phase and R&D teams aiming to commercialize their technologies.

Registration
Deadline

The deadline for technology registration is June 15, 2026.

Based on your application, our jury will select the most promising technologies to participate in the Pitch and Poster Sessions.

Pitch Session participants will present their technology and receive feedback from investors and the general audience. A dedicated Poster Session will also take place during the event.

Please note: The information submitted will be used for the selection process and may be published on our virtual platform and in the Conference book. Only share non-confidential information.

All attendees of Prague.bio Conference 2026 must purchase a ticket. Each registered technology will receive two discounted tickets for its representatives at EUR 200 + VAT per ticket.

For any questions, please contact us at conference@prague.bio.

Thank to our partners, we are pleased to announce the following prizes for the winners of the Pitch Session:

  1. 1
    1st placeEUR 3,000 + ticket to investment committee of United Founders VC + free ticket to BioVaria 2027
  2. 2
    2nd placeEUR 2,000 + free ticket to BioVaria 2027
  3. 3
    3rd placeEUR 1,000 + free ticket to BioVaria 2027

The awards are supported by IOCB Tech Foundation (cash prizes totaling EUR 6,000), United Founders VC (invitation to its Investment Committee, with the opportunity to secure an investment of up to EUR 1,000,000), and Ascenion (tickets to BioVaria 2027).
